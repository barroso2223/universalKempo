40+ Years Teaching
in Colorado Springs

Celebrating over four decades of teaching men, women, and children under the Universal Kempo‑Karate Schools Association — self defense, physical conditioning, and traditional values, carried forward from the system founded by Grandmaster Martin T. Buell.

Kempo Karate draws on Karate, Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, grappling, weapons, and boxing — one adaptable system, shaped to fit every student's size, strength, and ability.
The Universal Kempo-Karate curriculum

Training

One school. Every family member.

Self Defense

Realistic, adaptable technique drawn from Karate, Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, grappling, weapons, and boxing — tailored to every size, strength, and ability, not competition choreography.

Physical Conditioning

Rigorous, structured training that builds strength, endurance, and coordination — the same discipline that has defined this curriculum for over 40 years.

Traditional Values

Respect, focus, and family investment are part of the curriculum. Universal Kempo is a family-owned school, built to help other families grow together on the mat.
